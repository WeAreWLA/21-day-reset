Meet your nutritionist & behavioural change coach

Hi, I'm Anna, and I've been the woman starting over every Monday.

I used to be the biggest yo-yo dieter, jumping from one plan to the next, losing weight only to gain it back. I tried it all, laxatives, the cabbage soup diet, even that dreaded Special K challenge. I felt completely stuck.

That's what led me to study Food and Nutrition at university, a personal quest to finally understand how to fuel my body properly. What I discovered changed everything. I lost the weight, and I've never gained it back.

Today I'm a degree-qualified Registered Associate Nutritionist and have coached women for over 11 years, helping more than 50,000 break free from yo-yo dieting and feel amazing in their skin.

Life is for living, not being a slave to miserable diets. Change is possible.
