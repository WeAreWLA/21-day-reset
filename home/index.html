About Anna

I'm Anna — and I've been the woman starting over every Monday.

So I went back to university. Four years. A degree in Food & Nutrition. I became a Registered Associate Nutritionist and have spent over 10 years helping women get real, lasting results.

50,000 clients later, this is the simplest approach that actually gets results.
