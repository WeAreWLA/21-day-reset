Meet Anna Wallace

Founder of The Weight Loss Academy & Registered Associate Nutritionist.

I created the WLA to help women break free from yo-yo dieting and lose weight in a simple, sustainable way, so they can feel confident and in control around food again.

After years of struggling with emotional eating, "starting again on Monday" and chasing quick fixes, I made a promise to never diet again, and to learn how to nourish my body instead. That decision led me to study Food & Nutrition at university and become a qualified Nutritionist.

I developed our flexible, science-backed approach that has now helped over 50,000 women shift weight easily and sustainably. At the WLA, we don't do restriction, guilt, or low-calorie diets. We focus on simple nutrition, behaviour-change strategies and compassionate support that works with your life, not against it.

I'm passionate about showing women that their bodies aren't the problem. The diets are.

Our story

Founded by Anna Wallace, degree-qualified Registered Associate Nutritionist and former yo-yo dieter. The WLA helps women lose weight sustainably, reduce cravings and break free from self-sabotage, blending science, behaviour change and compassion so women feel balanced, confident and in control.

🎯

Our mission

To offer a simple, flexible alternative to restrictive diets. Through the WLA Nutrition Formula we focus on balance, sustainable habits and the freedom to enjoy all foods, in a supportive, judgement-free environment.

💛

Our ethos

We believe in nourishment over deprivation. Our approach pairs wholesome, family-friendly nutrition with behavioural-change tools to reduce emotional eating, build consistency and help women feel energised and confident in their bodies.
